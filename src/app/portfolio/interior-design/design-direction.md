# AUREL visual direction

A residential atelier that begins with everyday routines, the light of a building, and furniture worth keeping.

- Warm white paper (#f7f5ef), muted stone (#e9e5dc), and a single deep walnut accent (#513b2b).
- Editorial old-style serif headings paired with the existing Inter sans serif. Local serif fallbacks avoid another font download.
- A measured headline above a large landscape image. Captions sit outside photographs so the interiors remain unobstructed.
- An asymmetric featured project followed by two smaller projects, with visible locations, scope, and short design notes.
- Plain numbered process and specific working commitments rather than invented testimonials, metrics, or awards.
- A consistent “Discuss a project” action that leads to the existing contact page.
- Project names and locations are illustrative portfolio copy; the footer labels the concept and image provenance remains in image-sources.md.
- Mobile layouts stack naturally, with 44px minimum link targets, visible focus rings, and readable supporting text.
